Our story

PC Centre started as a small online platform linking tech enthusiasts with local dealers. Today it’s where gamers, students, professionals and businesses come for laptops, monitors, parts and accessories.

We built a network of authorized dealers in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi, so every product comes with a genuine warranty and support close to home.

  1. 2010PC Centre launches in Karachi as an online dealer directory.
  2. 2015Dealer network grows to Lahore and Islamabad.
  3. 2020Brand catalogues added for Dell, HP, Acer, Asus and Toshiba.
  4. 2025Online ordering with cart and WhatsApp checkout.
