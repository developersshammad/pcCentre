Help & FAQs

Quick answers about orders, warranty and our dealers.

Where are your support centres?

We have partner support centres in Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi. Addresses and phone numbers are listed on the catalogue page.

What warranty do products come with?

Most products include a 1-year manufacturer’s warranty. Some components, like SSDs and RAM, have longer warranties. The exact period is shown in each product’s specifications.

How do I return or exchange a product?

Contact our support team or the dealer within 7 days of purchase. Keep the invoice and original packaging, as both are needed for a return or exchange.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. We work with partner banks to offer installment plans on selected products. Ask your nearest dealer which plans are available for the product you want.

How do I track my order?

After you place an order, you’ll get a tracking number by SMS or email. Use it on the dealer’s website, or send it to us on WhatsApp for an update.

Can I pick up my order from a store?

Yes, most dealers offer in-store pickup. Call the dealer first to check the product is in stock.

How does ordering on WhatsApp work?

Add products to your cart and choose “Order on WhatsApp”. Your item list and total are sent to our team, who confirm stock, price and delivery with you.

Still need help?

Our team replies within a few hours, Monday to Saturday.
